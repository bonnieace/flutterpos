Daily trading report

The PDF sent on WhatsApp at 08:00 with the previous business date. Drawn in pt on A4, in main’s own colours and the Noto Sans the functions embed, so it carries straight into reportPdf.ts. Read README.md for the tokens, the rules the code must keep, and what’s new data rather than new drawing.

Page 1 — the day

The total and its three figures, the open-shift warning, sales per hour with the shifts on the same axis, how it was paid, and what sold by category.

A4

Page 2 — the items

Every item under its category in two columns, with size, price, quantity and amount. A real Thamani day fits on one page.

A4

A day with no sales

Still sent, so silence never means the report broke; says plainly what an empty day can mean.
