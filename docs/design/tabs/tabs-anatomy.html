Tabs — anatomy

AppTabs now draws one of two shapes, chosen by how many tabs there are. Two tabs get a switch, after the Live / Demo control in the crypto wallet reference: a capsule track with a raised thumb behind the selected half. Three or more get icon tabs, after the action dashboard reference: a glyph and a label per tab, with the selected one in the accent and underlined. Both are still TabBar underneath, with the same controller, taps, swipes and semantics.

Which screens get which

The count decides, so no screen has to choose. Configs has three tabs or four depending on whether loyalty is wired, and it is icon tabs either way.

  • On two-tab screens, selection is a shape, not a 3px line. The thumb sits behind the selected half. Its edge is 1.83:1 against the track, so the control reads as two options with one chosen, which is the reference’s whole idea.
  • On Configs, each tab carries the glyph its own panel already uses (payments, assignment, handshake, loyalty). The selected tab is green in glyph, label and underline. The others are text.tertiary, 1.87:1 below it, where today the two label colours are 1.31:1 apart.
  • Fixes carried over from the first pass: no 52px gap before a scrolling strip, a 32 fade on the end with more tabs, a 48 strip where today’s is 50, a 2px underline where Material 3 draws 3, and a layout measured at the current text size.
  • On a wide screen, the switch is as wide as its labels need, not the column’s 680, so its two halves stay a thumb’s width apart.

Measurements

Twice size. Every figure is on the spacing scale or is a component constant the app already has. At 3x, the reference dashboard measures 9 from glyph to label and 23 between tabs. Those snap to space.8 and two space.12s.

48 4 + 16 thumb: inset 4, pill wash: inset 4, pill ring: 2 at 2 offset
48 12 18 8 12 2 underline, r 2 wash: inset 4, r 8 ring: 2 at 2 offset
  • Both are 48 high: Tab(height: 46) plus indicatorWeight 2, so the strip and the target are the 48 that AppTabs.height already claims.
  • The switch: the thumb is a BoxDecoration indicator at TabBarIndicatorSize.tab with indicatorPadding 4. A label sits 4 + 16 from its half’s edge. The focus ring follows the thumb’s shape at a 2 offset, so its outside is the track’s edge.
  • Icon tabs: space.12, glyph 18, space.8, label, space.12. The underline is TabBarIndicatorSize.label, which measures the tab’s whole child, so it spans the glyph and the label, as the reference’s does.
  • On a page, the icon strip is padded by 8: the page inset of 20, less the tab’s 12. That lines the first glyph up with the text below. A switch on a page sits inside the page’s own inset instead.

Layout by width

AppTabs decides this itself, measuring labels at the current text size.

Switch · below 768

Across the column

The track fills the content width and each half is a wide target, as the reference draws it. At large text a label wraps onto a second line and the track grows.

Switch · 768 and up

As wide as its labels

Each half is as wide as the wider label plus space.24 each side, so the halves stay equal. The switch sits at the start of the column.

Icon tabs · any width

Together from the start, scrolling if needed

As in the reference, the tabs are never stretched to equal widths. Where they do not fit, the strip scrolls from the start and the end with more tabs fades over 32.

RuleHow both answer it
48dpEvery segment and every tab is 48 high and at least 48 wide.
Not colour aloneThe switch shows selection with a shape (the thumb), a brighter label, and selected to a screen reader. Icon tabs show it with the underline, the accent, and selected.
FocusThe app’s ring, 2px at a 2px offset, round the segment’s or the tab’s shape. Tab and the arrow keys move focus; Enter or Space selects. That is Flutter’s default traversal, unchanged.
NamesTabBar’s own semantics, unchanged: role tab, selected, Tab 1 of 2. Glyphs are decorative and add nothing to the name.
Large textThe switch wraps a label onto two lines and grows. If a single word still cannot fit, it falls back to the underline strip without glyphs, which scrolls. Icon tabs scroll rather than cut.
