States

How the page behaves while it reads, when part of it cannot be read, while a payment is still on the phone, and in the form it takes before invoices exist. That last form is what Phase A would build first.

Reading, failing, waiting to send

The account comes from the customer’s own record and arrives with it. The tabs read separately. A tab that cannot be read costs that tab only, as Purchases does today.

Loading keeps the page’s shape. Today it shows Reading this customer and a spinner. Skeleton bars in the account’s layout mean nothing jumps when it arrives. The toolbar says Customer until the name is read, as today.
A failed tab says what is still true. ErrorState’s own pattern: a Not read badge, what failed, and that the figures above are unaffected, because they come from a different read.
A payment on the phone is said, not guessed. The figures stay the server’s; the callout names the amount and its receipt. It needs the page to read the pending-call queue for this customer, which no screen does today. That is a new read, not a restyle, and yours to keep or drop.

Before invoices, on the first day, and blocked

The page degrades by phase. Phase A ships held money, voids and receipts before any invoice exists. The page then has no ageing and no Invoices tab, and is otherwise the same design. On go-live day everything owed is Before invoicing.

Phase A’s page. One figure and Owing since, as today, because there is no due date to age against. The held half is already there, holding the money Phase A stops spending automatically: the row says the credit sale of 14 Oct did not use it, because today it would have. Two tabs make the switch, as today.
Go-live day. The bar is one hatched segment and the Invoices tab holds one pinned row. Its foot says what changes from today, so the first manager to open it is not left wondering where the invoices are. Cents are shown where the figure has them (the no-.00 ruling holds).
Inactive and blocked. Today’s inactive callout, unchanged. A credit block shows in the terms line with a block glyph and the words; it is not a badge, because nothing on this page resolves it (it is changed in Edit). Record a payment drops to secondary on an inactive customer but stays: they can still pay what they owe.
