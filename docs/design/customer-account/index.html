Customer account

A customer’s page once quotes, orders and invoices exist (ACCOUNTING_WORKFLOW_DISCOVERY.md). It is opened to answer one question: where do things stand with this person? What they owe and how old it is, what of theirs you are holding, and what needs doing. Debt and held money are shown side by side and never netted. Every action says what it will change before it changes it. Read README.md for the reasoning, what the spec is missing, and the open questions.
