Quotes

A new Sales icon (D-42). A quote is an offer with a number, a revision and a day it lasts until. It is built with the till’s own product search, works with no shift open, goes out on WhatsApp, by email or as a PDF, and ends as a sale, an order, or nothing. Read README.md for the reasoning and the open questions.
