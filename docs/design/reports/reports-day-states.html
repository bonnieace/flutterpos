Day — what the takings card can say

Every state below is a real one the data model produces. TenantDailyMetricsStanding has four values and its own doc comment says “today is normally” unpublished — so the common case for a Day report is the one with no certified figure in it.

What was left out, and why

No hourly curve. Nothing in daily_metrics or on a sale carries time-of-day in a form anything aggregates. Drawing one would mean inventing it. Across the day uses shifts instead, which are the day’s real buckets and carry a per-shift total already.

No top items for a day. The week gets its best-sellers from a capped sale-line walk. Paying that on every Day open, for the report most likely to be opened several times a day, is the wrong trade — and the expand arrow on a card is where it belongs if it is wanted.

No target or comparison. There is no per-day target field anywhere in the model, and yesterday is a second read. A vs yesterday line is one extra document read if you want it — worth deciding deliberately rather than assuming.
