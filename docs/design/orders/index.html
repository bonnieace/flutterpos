Orders

A new Sales icon (D-42). An order is an accepted job that is not handed over in one go: it holds its deposit, fixes its prices, and counts what was ordered, delivered and cancelled on each line. Every delivery is a sale with its own invoice. Read README.md for the reasoning and the open questions.
