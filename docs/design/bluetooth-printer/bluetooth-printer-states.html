Bluetooth printer — states

Every state the Bluetooth tab can be in, each at the phone column’s own width (371, a 411 screen less two 20 insets). Sentences the build already says are quoted unchanged and marked built; sentences these boards add are marked new. Under each, the field or answer that produces it.

A search

Android’s discovery runs about twelve seconds; the list fills as it hears. The search button is primary only while it is the one thing to do.

Every answer

One snack bar, replaced rather than queued — or, while a printer’s sheet is open, the same sentence in a callout in the sheet, under its title: a snack bar is drawn by the page, and the page is under the sheet. Sentences from PrinterFailureNotice are the same ones a failed receipt shows in the print queue, so the till and this page never word one fault twice.
