Motion

One thing moves: the page figure, through RollingNumber at spring.snappy (mass 1, stiffness 380, damping 32). It rolls the way the figure moved, only changed digits move, a retrigger picks up from the current position and velocity, and with animations off it swaps. The press is PressSurface’s own. Nothing waits for the roll: the callback has already fired.

Which records these are

“Page 4” says where; “61–80” says how far in. At the start of the row on a monitor, above the pager and start-aligned on a phone. There is no of N, and never will be from these reads: they page by cursor and do not learn a total.

New inputs: the first and last record numbers. The call site holds pageSize and items.length.

P3 · needs end-of-list apart from paused

The end is said in words, and a single page has no bar

On the last page the position says last, so a disabled Next is explained in words rather than by its colour. A list that fits on one page draws nothing to page with. The bar cannot move under a thumb when nothing on it can be pressed.

New input: endOfList, from hasMore. Today it is folded into a null onNext together with “paused”.
