Pagination — anatomy

The bar under seventeen record lists, redrawn as one pager: the way back, where you are, and the way on, joined in a single container that sits at the end of the list on a monitor and takes the row on a phone. Same three inputs, same three keys, same callbacks.

Before and after

The bar as the harness captures it today, against the redesign, at the two widths it spends most of its life at. Both are the first page of a list with more to come.

Measurements

Twice size. Every figure is on the spacing scale or is a component constant the app already has.

Page 4 48 16 18 8 16 r 8 1px divider 4 focus gutter
  • Group 48 high: AppButtonSize.lg, the only size §5.2 gives a touch surface, and the 48dp target with nothing added.
  • Nav segment: 16 + glyph 18 + 8 + word + 16 — AppButtonSize.horizontalPadding(hasIcon: true), AppButton.iconSize, space.8. Never narrower than 48.
  • Outer corners 8 (radius.md, as every button); inner corners square; dividers 1, painted rather than laid out, as AppButton’s border is.
  • The dashed line is PressSurface’s 4 focus-ring gutter, reserved round the group whether or not a ring is showing — which is why the group’s outer edge sits 4 in from the list’s edge above it.
