The photo sheet

Opened by ⋯ or by tapping the tile. Wider screens

From 768 the build puts the photo in a column beside the fields, 2 to 3, with the picture up to 350 tall. The proposal keeps one column at the 720 reading width, with the same photo row on top, and lets the prices and the code share a line.
