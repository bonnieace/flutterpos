Quote, invoice, and an invoice paid by its deposit

Phase C’s A4 renderer, drawn at the PDF reports’ own geometry: 595 × 842 points, 40-point sides and 36-point top and bottom, Hanken Grotesk, and the report palette’s ink, rule, wash and accent (lib/reports/report_metrics.dart, AppReportRoles). One page per document. These are the files a customer receives on WhatsApp or by email, and the preview on a wide screen.

A quote and an invoice

QT-0019, revision 2, as Kamau Njoroge received it; INV-0014, St. Mark’s first delivery, with the deposit applied.

One letterhead, every document. The logo, name and slogan from Business details; the phone, address and KRA PIN from the new letterhead fields (D-21). A 2-point accent rule closes it, as the report PDFs’ title band does.
The title is the document’s kind, plain: Quote, Invoice, Order, Payment receipt. Never Tax invoice (D-04). The quote’s footer says it is not an invoice.
The boxed figure is what the reader must act on: the quote’s total, the invoice’s amount due. Above it, every step from total to that figure is a printed line: a discount, a deposit applied, credit used.
A discounted price shows its list price under it in small type. That is the quote’s selling point, and the same agreed-price arithmetic the till records (D-08).
Payment instructions are a box from the letterhead (D-21). On an invoice the Paybill account is the invoice number, so a payment arrives already quoting it.
An order delivery’s invoice says where the order stands: ordered, delivered and deposit left. A customer paying in parts can see what is coming.

A delivery the deposit paid in full

Achieng Atieno’s first office-tea delivery, 4 November: 300 mandazi at 20, paid entirely from her 8,000 deposit. D-07: every delivery gets an invoice, and this one shows Paid. It reads the same whichever reached the server first, the deposit or the sale.

Paid is a stamp and a zero, never only a colour: the boxed figure reads 0.00 and the word Paid stands under it, in the accent green on white (8.4:1). The report palette’s brighter green is 3.0:1 on paper, too light for an 8-point word.
The deposit box tells the customer what is left for the next delivery. That is the question a deposit-paying customer asks, and the order holds the answer (§9 depositHeldMinor).
Issued and due dates still print on a paid invoice, for the customer’s own books. The due date is the shop’s 7-day default (D-07).
