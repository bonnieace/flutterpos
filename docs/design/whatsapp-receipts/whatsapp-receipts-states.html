WhatsApp receipts — states

Every state the two tabs can be in, at the phone column’s width (379, a 411 screen less two 16 insets). Every sentence is the build’s own and quoted unchanged; what these boards change is how it is drawn. Under each, the field or answer that produces it.

Every status, as ticks and a word

WhatsApp’s marks, because they are the ones the customer already reads on their own phone. The word goes with every one: two ticks differ from two ticks only by colour, and a status is never colour alone.
