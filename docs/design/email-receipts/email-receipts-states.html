Email receipts — states

Every state the two tabs can be in, at the phone column’s width (379, a 411 screen less two 16 insets). Every sentence is the build’s own or the server’s, quoted unchanged; what these boards change is how it is drawn. Under each, the field or answer that produces it.

Every status, as a glyph and a word

Mail’s own marks: a clock for a message still going out, the paper plane of a Sent folder, and the inbox tray it lands in. The word goes with every one, so a status is never colour alone and never a glyph alone. There is no Read: UzaPay’s emails carry no tracking pixel, so nobody knows.

Not sent · skipped

Not sent takes the quietest ink a person is still expected to read: a customer who stopped receipts, or receipts switched off, is the system working. WhatsApp’s tab draws it the same.

A sent receipt
