Email receipts screen

Three artboards for both tabs of TenantEmailReceiptsScreen. Sent receipts is laid out the way Gmail lays out an inbox, with a letter avatar and three lines per message: who and when, the subject, then the snippet. Settings follows the WhatsApp receipts screen as built. Both use this app’s own tokens, not Gmail’s colours. Read README.md for what changes, what was left out, and what is still open.

Settings — phone

The preview first, as the unread message the customer sees in their inbox, with the PDF as a chip. Then WhatsApp’s switch and flat timing rows, with no format to choose. Drawn on, read-only, and off.
