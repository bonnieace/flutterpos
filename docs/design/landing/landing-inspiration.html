What the inspiration gave, and what it could not

The brief names mobilesasa.com as the reference. It is a good one — same market, same buyer, same currency, and a page that has clearly been earning its keep for years. Its shape is worth taking almost whole.

What does not transfer is its evidence. Mobile Sasa opens on four numbers — 2,000+ organisations, <5 sec delivery, 99.9% uptime, in production since 2015 — then closes with forty client logos and three testimonials. That is the strongest part of the page and this product has none of it. Every one of those devices is on the brief’s do not claim list, and rightly: a beta with no public customers that prints an uptime figure has told its first lie above the fold.

So the structure is borrowed and the proof is rebuilt out of things that are true today.

Where the paper came from

The first draft of this page was dark green throughout, with two light bands — reconciliation and pricing — carrying the figures. Those two were the ones that worked, and the page is now paper end to end.

That is Mobile Sasa’s ground, and taking it did not mean inventing a palette. AppReportRoles already is one: paperWhite, paperWash, paperInk, paperMutedInk, paperRule and the eight chart hues, the ground every report and chart in the app is drawn on. White and wash alternate to break the scroll, and nothing on the page is a colour the product did not already own.

Two things stay dark, and both earn it. The phone in the hero, because that is the product and a dark device against white is the oldest trick there is for making a screenshot the thing you look at. And the closing band, so the last thing on screen is the app’s own world rather than another white form.

The primary button went the other way. Mobile Sasa’s CTA is a saturated blue; the obvious move here was a saturated green. It reads consumer — an app you download, not a system you run your shop on. Near‑black ink (#15251B) is the serious register the brief asks for, and it leaves the green to do the work it is better at: kickers, ticks, and the day that checks out.
