The night, case by case

The track runs from the first open shift's start to the moment it stops selling: 06:00, or 24 hours after it opened if that comes first. The fill is what has gone by. The notch is midnight, the line between the two calendar days. Its 00:00 label is dropped when it would sit within 20% of either end, and the notch stays.
