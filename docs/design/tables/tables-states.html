States

Every state a row and a sheet can be in, drawn on Suppliers, whose rows reach all of them. The states of each control inside a row, such as the button and the menu, are the components’ own and are not redrawn here.

The whole row is one PressSurface that opens the record. The kebab is a second focus stop inside it. Fills come from the surface roles, never from a tint of the action colour, so every ink keeps its contrast on every state.

Focus is drawn inside the row. AppFocusRing is 2 wide with a 2 offset. Outside the row, the rows above and below would paint over it, so the ring is inset by its own offset, inside the 4 gutter PressSurface already reserves. Tab moves row, then kebab, then the next row. Enter opens the row. Arrow keys between rows would be new, and are an open question.
Inactive keeps its row. The name and every figure step down to text.tertiary, and the neutral Inactive badge says why, so the state is never told by greyness alone. The row still opens and the menu offers Reactivate. It appears only under the Inactive or All filter, as today.
Not sent yet is a caption in place of the identity’s second line, with the upload glyph in status.attention. It appears only where a screen already knows a record is still on the device, as the sales outbox does. Whether the supplier list knows that about a queued supplier today is an open question in the README. No screen gains a sync check to draw it.

A sheet

The sheet keeps its frame through every state, so a list that is loading, empty or failing still reads as the same object in the same place. The bar stays wherever its controls still mean something.
