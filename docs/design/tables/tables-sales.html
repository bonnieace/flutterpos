Today
One card per receipt at every width; its own comment says it “draws cards at every width”. Every line of every sale is always shown, so a page of 20 is about 3,000 pixels tall.
Now
One 56 row per receipt. The lines fold under it, and the row itself is the fold: today a tap on the card does nothing. The till receipt (CT-8F8-0045) moves to the row’s screen-reader label and the menu tooltip, where it already is.
Unchanged
The menu (Return, Reprint receipt, Send on WhatsApp, Send by email, Set or change payment method) and its gates; the search, date and Sold-by filters; 20 per page; net totals and the Partly returned badge; the deliberate silence about how much was returned.

Oldest first. A row opens that shift’s sales, narrowed to its cashier.
Today
A 205-tall card per shift, with a day heading. A difference is drawn only as “Over by” or “Short by” in red.
Now
A signed difference in its own column, with a real minus. The standing badge carries the colour, so the figure does not have to. An amended shift shows corrected ×1 under its recorded sales, and its menu holds This shift’s corrections, the button the card had.
Unchanged
The row opens the Sales Report on that shift’s day and cashier; oldest first; the date and staff filters; 20 per page; the five standings.

Verify
A pending return is already off the takings. Its stock comes back when an owner, administrator or manager verifies it.
Today
Cards with a glyph tile, an upper-case PENDING or VERIFIED in bare status colour, “Sold 11:01 → Returned 14:30”, and two icon buttons: view, and verify.
Now
The row opens the return’s journal, which the eye button did. Verify is a labelled button rather than an icon. The status is a StatusBadge rather than coloured capitals. The pending sentence is said once, in the foot, instead of on every card.
Unchanged
Who may verify, and the confirmation it asks for; the day and standing filters; grouping by day; 20 per page.

Notifications stays a list

It is not a list of records. Failed print jobs, counts of sales waiting to send, and shifts left open are three different things with three different actions, and a table would give them columns they do not share. What it needs at 768 and up is the reading width (720) instead of 1200, so its sentences stop running across the screen. That is a one-line change, and it is not drawn here.
