Widths

One day of Shift history, as a manager sees it, drawn at five widths. From 1024 the sheet is the full table. From 768 it is the same table with two columns folded into the identity’s second line. Below 768 it can become a list, which is offered rather than proposed: phones were not part of the brief. Frames are drawn at their real heights, so what is below the fold is below the fold.

What decides a column’s width

Figures are measured, not guessed. A figure, code, time or status column is as wide as its widest value on the page or its header, whichever is wider. That is RecordTableColumns’ job today, and it already measures through measureTextWidth with the ambient letter spacing.
Text shares what is left. Identity, text and person columns divide the remainder by the shares a screen gives them. The identity always gets the largest share, and it is the one that clips.
Priority, not a fourth breakpoint. A column declares always, wide or full. At medium only always and wide columns stay, and the rest fold into the caption in the order declared. The bands are the two the app has: 768 and 1024.
