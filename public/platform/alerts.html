Platform operations

Platform alerts live in the app

There is no web console. Open ChillTent as a platform administrator and go to the platform section.

The email that sent you here holds the detail of the alert itself; this page exists so that link resolves to something rather than nowhere.

Access

Alerts are only visible to accounts holding a platform administrator grant.

Nothing about a tenant, an account or the reason for an alert is ever sent by email.
