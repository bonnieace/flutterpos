Privacy policy

How UzaPay handles your data

UzaPay is a point-of-sale app for shops. This policy says what the app collects, why, who processes it, and how to have it deleted.

UzaPay is operated by Boniface Masota, Kenya. Questions about this policy or your data go to support@uzapay.co.ke.

What we collect

  • Your account: your full name, email address, mobile number and password. The password is held by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
  • Your staff: the name, email address and role of anyone you invite to your business.
  • Your business records: business name and details, products, prices, stock, sales and how they were paid, returns, expenses, cash counts, shifts, suppliers and their contact details, and production records.
  • Photos: product photos and a business logo, if you add them.
  • Device details: an identifier for each phone or tablet used as a till, so each sale and shift can name the device it was made on.
  • Error reports: when something goes wrong, the app sends a report containing the error, the steps that led to it, your user ID, name, email address and business name. Screen recordings are never taken, and passwords, tokens and customer details are removed before a report leaves the device.

UzaPay does not collect your location, contacts, messages or anything about your customers beyond what a sale records. It shows no ads and does not sell or rent data to anyone.

Why we use it

To run the app: to sign you in, keep your business's records and share them with the staff you invite, send account emails (verification, password reset, invitations), and find and fix faults. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Who processes it for us

Data is sent over encrypted connections to these services, which process it only to provide the app:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) stores your account and business records and photos. Its servers are in the United States.
  • Brevo sends account emails to the addresses involved.
  • remove.bg (Kaleido AI) receives a product photo only when you ask the app to remove its background.
  • Error reports go to a GlitchTip server operated by UzaPay itself.

Receipts are sent directly from your device to the printer you set up on your own network.

How long we keep it

Business records are kept while the business has an active account, so its history stays available to the business. Error reports are kept for up to 90 days. When an account is deleted, its data is removed as described on the account deletion page.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how it is used. Email support@uzapay.co.ke from the address on your account and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

UzaPay is a business tool for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
